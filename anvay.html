The piece

Anvay, meaning connection and continuity, is conceived as a dining composition where material, proportion and form come together in quiet harmony. A bold black stone tabletop anchors the set with a grounded presence, while the warm timber structure and woven chair backs introduce softness, rhythm and craft. The gently rounded table profile and curved chair frames create a subtle sense of flow, allowing each element to feel distinct yet visually connected. Balanced between solidity and lightness, Anvay reflects the spirit of Pravaah — a place where people, materials and moments come together as one continuous experience.
