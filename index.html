Pravaah — flow

Stillness within movement.

Pravaah explores the quiet continuity found in nature — where movement shapes form without force, and stillness exists within the flow. Inspired by the gentle rhythm of water, shifting landscapes and organic transitions, the collection reflects the balance between movement and pause.

Rather than capturing movement itself, Pravaah captures the stillness within movement and the trace it leaves behind.
