The piece

Laya, meaning rhythm or cadence, reflects the gentle repetition of movement that defines a swing. Within the Pravaah collection, it becomes an expression of motion held in balance — where each forward and backward movement returns naturally to a moment of stillness. Its form is conceived through soft curves, continuous structural lines and a calm, suspended presence. Warm wood brings depth and craftsmanship, refined upholstery adds comfort, and subtle champagne-gold PVD accents introduce a restrained sense of luxury. Laya is designed as more than a suspended seat; it is a quiet rhythm in space — fluid in movement, composed in form, and serene in presence.
