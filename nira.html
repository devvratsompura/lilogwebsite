The piece

Nira evokes the quiet presence of water — calm on the surface, yet carrying an effortless sense of movement within. Conceived as the sofa of the Pravaah collection, its form is shaped through gentle curves, continuous lines and softened transitions that allow the structure to flow naturally around the body. Grounded proportions give Nira a feeling of stillness, while its sweeping silhouette introduces a subtle sense of motion. Tactile upholstery, sculpted timber and carefully resolved details come together as one harmonious expression — creating a sofa that feels serene, fluid, embracing and timeless.

Detail

A back woven in cord

The back of the sofa is framed in warm timber and finished with a hand-woven Danish cord panel, introducing a tactile layer of craft to the otherwise soft upholstered composition. The closely spaced vertical weave creates a subtle rhythm and visual transparency, allowing the back to feel lighter while still providing flexible support.
