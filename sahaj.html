The piece

Sahaj, meaning effortless and natural, reflects the quiet ease at the heart of the Pravaah collection. Its sculptural wooden frame flows seamlessly around the body, creating a form that feels composed, balanced and instinctive rather than deliberately constructed. Fine upholstery softens the structure and brings a sense of comfort and tactility, while subtle brass accents introduce a refined contrast against the warmth of the wood. Together, these materials create a harmonious balance of craft, comfort and understated luxury — giving Sahaj a presence that is fluid, grounded and timeless.
