Spaces

Furniture considered as part of architecture.

Rooms drawn around single pieces. The distance between an object and a wall is treated as part of the design.

01 — Living

Read from every side.

Nira is set forward of the plaster wall so its woven back is part of the room rather than hidden against it — the piece can be walked around, and the cord panel keeps the volume feeling light.

Furniture
Nira seating, Spand centre table
Materials
Woven fabric, solid timber, Danish cord
Light
Full-height glazing, soft daylight

02 — Dining

Where things come together.

A bold black stone tabletop anchors the room, while the warm timber structure and woven chair backs introduce softness, rhythm and craft — solidity and lightness held in one composition.

Furniture
Anvay dining set
Materials
Black stone, solid timber, woven backs
Light
Garden-facing, late afternoon

03 — Lounge

Objects in the round.

Nothing touches a wall. Spand sits low between the seating, its glass plane reading as a still surface of water, while Laya introduces movement to the corner of the room.

Furniture
Spand centre table, Laya swing, Sahaj armchair
Materials
Glass, timber, champagne-gold PVD
Light
Double-height volume
