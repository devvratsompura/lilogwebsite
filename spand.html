Centre Table — Pravaah Collection

Spand

A pulse of glass, held between sculptural timber forms.

Enquire

The piece

Spand, meaning pulse or subtle vibration, captures the quiet rhythm that exists within the Pravaah collection. Its composition is built around a transparent glass plane that appears to float between sculptural wooden forms, creating a sense of lightness, movement and layered depth. The varying heights and rounded timber elements establish a gentle visual rhythm, while the champagne-gold PVD detailing introduces a refined metallic accent against the warmth of the wood. Glass, timber and metal come together as distinct materials yet remain visually connected — giving Spand a character that feels dynamic yet balanced, sculptural yet calm.

Detail

Still flow

The centre table is conceived as a quiet expression of still flow, where the circular glass top recalls the calm surface of water held within a central pond. Smooth, reflective and visually light, the glass introduces a sense of stillness, while the sculpted wooden base beneath grounds the piece with warmth and form.
