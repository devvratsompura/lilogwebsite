The Studio

We design furniture with the space around it in mind.

Philosophy

Pravaah — flow.

Pravaah, meaning flow, explores the quiet continuity found in nature — where movement shapes form without force, and stillness exists within the flow. Inspired by the gentle rhythm of water, shifting landscapes and organic transitions, the collection reflects the balance between movement and pause, translating this harmony into sculptural furniture.

Like water that can move yet remain profoundly still, Pravaah carries a sense of calm within its fluidity. Rather than capturing movement itself, it captures the stillness within movement and the trace it leaves behind — creating furniture that feels fluid yet grounded, expressive yet serene, and timeless in its presence.

Design

Proportion, geometry, comfort and function.

Each piece is defined by flowing silhouettes, seamless transitions, considered proportions and tactile materials, allowing structure, craft and comfort to exist as one continuous expression. Comfort is resolved inside that discipline rather than added to it — a seat height, a back angle and a curve of the frame are decisions about geometry before they are decisions about softness.

Material

Surface quality and tactile experience.

The palette is deliberately narrow: sculpted timber, tactile upholstery, hand-woven Danish cord, glass, black stone, brass and champagne-gold PVD. Distinct materials are chosen so they remain visually connected — each selected for how it holds light across a day, and how it ages over a decade.
